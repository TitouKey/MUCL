Subject: Money without borders. Exploration without borders.

Dear Revolut team,

Somewhere between Brussels and the Utah desert, there is a crew that needs its
money to travel with them. Flights, car rental, gear, a thousand small
unforeseen costs in a country on the other side of the Atlantic. For a
generation that lives without borders, Revolut is not an app. It is the
passport. One card, every currency, no hidden fees, no one telling you that
moving money around the world has to be painful.

Revolut was built on a quiet revolution: that money should move as freely as
people do. That a student crossing borders should not be punished for it. That
financial freedom is not a privilege of the wealthy, but a tool for the
curious. Millions of young Europeans learned that their first adventure would
not be stopped by their bank — and that is a gift this generation will not
forget.

We are MARS UCLouvain, a student space programme from UCLouvain in Belgium.
Every two years, we send a crew of students to the Mars Desert Research
Station in the Utah desert — one of the most faithful Mars analogues on Earth.
For two weeks they live in isolation, run real experiments in health, biology,
geology and technology, and bring the mission back to classrooms across the
country. Since 2013, dozens of research projects have crossed the Atlantic
with us.

Our mission is a story of borders crossed. A continent crossed by plane. An
ocean crossed for a desert that looks like another planet. And a frontier
crossed by young people who decided that exploration belonged to them. You
made money borderless; we make exploration borderless. Your card is the quiet
partner that makes our expedition possible — one card, every country, every
expense, all the way to the red dust.

And we want to bring your story into our classrooms. Because explorers need
more than courage: they need to know how to manage the resources of their
adventure — and of their life. A workshop on financial literacy, delivered by
our crew to students across the country, with Revolut as the example that
money can be simple, fair and free.

Our challenge to you: be the bank of the mission. Open the expedition's
account and put your card in the crew's hands, from the first training day to
the last night in the desert. In return, your name travels on our mission
jersey, your story reaches our classrooms, and a generation of young
Europeans learns the two lessons you and we both believe in: money without
borders, and the courage to go.

Take us across the Atlantic. We'll bring back the story.
