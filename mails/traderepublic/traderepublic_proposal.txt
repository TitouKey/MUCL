Subject: The best investment is the one you can't touch. Ours is a mission.

Dear Trade Republic team,

Somewhere in Europe, a generation is learning something its parents never
learned: that a small amount of money, started early and left alone, quietly
becomes something enormous. Trade Republic taught them. You took investing out
of the marble halls and put it in a pocket — fractional shares, saving plans,
a few euros a month, and time doing the heavy lifting. You understood that the
biggest fortunes are not built by the loudest bets, but by patience.

We are here to talk about the same thing: the long game.

We are MARS UCLouvain, a student space programme from UCLouvain in Belgium.
Every two years, we send a crew of students to the Mars Desert Research
Station in the Utah desert — one of the most faithful Mars analogues on Earth.
For two weeks they live in isolation, run real experiments in health, biology,
geology and technology, and bring the mission back to classrooms across the
country. Since 2013, dozens of research projects have crossed the Atlantic
with us.

Look at our mission the way you look at an investment. Years of training for
two weeks in the desert. Experiments that may not pay off. Failures absorbed
quietly, lessons compounded. And a return that no one can price: a generation
of students who have touched exploration and will carry it for a lifetime.
Exploration is the compound interest of humanity. Every mission adds to the
principal. And the young people who run ours are the ones who will one day
manage the return.

You democratised investing. We are democratising exploration — our crew is not
a team of elites; it is a team of students from every faculty, every
background, with a small budget and a very big horizon. Same bet, different
currency: that the future is worth betting on, and that starting small does
not mean aiming small.

Our challenge to you: be the financial partner of the mission. Fund a part of
our expedition — and, in exchange, help us teach our students the other
investment they will need for life: how to make their own money work, one
euro at a time. Your name on our mission jersey, your story in our
classrooms, and a generation that understands that investing — in markets, in
ideas, in people — is what moves humanity forward.

Some people invest in stocks. We invest in the future. Join us.
