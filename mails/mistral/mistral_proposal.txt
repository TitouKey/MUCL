Subject: There is more than one way to build the future. Europe's proof is you.

Dear Mistral team,

When the world says artificial intelligence, it usually means a handful of
names on the other side of the Atlantic. OpenAI. Anthropic. The giants that
feel like the whole story. But the whole story is bigger — and a part of it is
being written in Paris, by a company barely a few years old, built for one
purpose: to prove that Europe can lead. That company is you.

Mistral was born from a conviction we share to the letter: Europe can do great
things. Sovereign AI. Models that answer to the values of their continent, and
to a generation that should never have to believe the future is only built
elsewhere. You gave young Europeans a reason to stay, to build, to believe
that being at the top does not require crossing an ocean. That is not just a
company position. That is a message we want to carry into every classroom we
enter.

We are MARS UCLouvain, a student space programme from UCLouvain in Belgium.
Every two years, we send a crew of students to the Mars Desert Research
Station in the Utah desert — one of the most faithful Mars analogues on Earth.
For two weeks they live in isolation, run real experiments in health, biology,
geology and technology, and bring the mission back to classrooms across the
country. Since 2013, dozens of research projects have crossed the Atlantic
with us.

We are not building AI. We are building the same thing you are: European
champions. On our side, champions of exploration. And in the desert, a crew
needs intelligence on board. Analysing research data, drafting mission
reports, translating our discoveries into lessons for schools, keeping
students connected to knowledge when they are 8,000 kilometres from home.
That is where Le Chat belongs: on our mission, proving live, in the harshest
environment a student crew can reach, that European AI is real — and that it
works.

Our request to you: give our crew access to your software for the duration of
the partnership. Let Le Chat work beside our experiments in the Utah desert
and in our classrooms across Belgium. Let every student who uses it hear the
same lesson we want to teach: when you ask an AI for help, it does not have to
come from California. It can come from Europe. It can be built in Europe. And
it can be something we are proud of.

Our challenge to you: be the AI of the mission. Help us prove, from the red
desert, that Europe's champions are not a promise for tomorrow — they are
here, today, building the future with us. And in every classroom, we will
tell the next generation the same words you once told the world: you can do
great things. Here. In Europe. Just like Mistral.

[Your name] — MARS UCLouvain
