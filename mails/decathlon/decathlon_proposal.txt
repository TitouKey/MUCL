Subject: The tent from our first camp. The brand of our first adventures. Ready for Mars?

Dear Décathlon team,

There is a tent that stands in the memory of almost every young adventurer.
The one from the first scout camp, pitched in the rain, forever impossible to
fold back into its bag. There is a headlamp that survived every night of every
festival. A fleece that went everywhere. A chair that has known more fields
than we can count. For millions of us, adventure did not begin with a
mountaineer on a summit — it began in a Décathlon aisle, picking the first
piece of gear that made us believe we could go.

Décathlon made adventure accessible. Not reserved for the wealthy, not for the
already-daring — accessible. A first camp, a first festival, a first hike, a
first night under the stars with gear that costs less than a night out. You
gave a whole generation the quiet confidence that the world was open to them.
You were there before the adventure was even cool. You were there at the very
first step.

We are MARS UCLouvain, a student space programme from UCLouvain in Belgium.
Every two years, we send a crew of students to the Mars Desert Research
Station in the Utah desert — one of the most faithful Mars analogues on Earth.
For two weeks they live in isolation, run real experiments in health, biology,
geology and technology, and bring the mission back to classrooms across the
country. Since 2013, dozens of research projects have crossed the Atlantic
with us.

Our mission is your philosophy, taken one step further. Space has always been
told as the most inaccessible adventure of all — the dream of the few, the
elite, the extraordinary. We believe the opposite: that exploration is for
everyone, and that a student from any faculty, any background, any bank
account, can go explore. Our crew is not a team of superheroes. It is a team
of students — the same people you used to see in the camping aisle, choosing
their first tent. The desert is, in the end, a very big scout camp.

So here is our question. You have accompanied us everywhere — the camps, the
festivals, the mountains, the first adventures of our lives. But are you ready
to go to Mars?

Our challenge to you: equip our crew for the desert. Tents that hold against
the wind, sleeping bags for nights that freeze, backpacks that survive two
weeks of red dust, boots that know the way. Take the brand of our childhoods
and plant it on the red soil of the most Mars-like place on Earth. In return,
your name travels on our mission jersey, in our classrooms, and in the story
we bring back: that the company that made adventure accessible to everyone was
the one that followed a student crew to the edge of the map.

The same tent that sheltered our first camps, pitched on another planet.
Are you ready?

[Your name] — MARS UCLouvain
